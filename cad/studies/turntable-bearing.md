These are visual estimates, not calibrated measurements. Four holes per ring are visible; the outer holes have countersinks. Nominal 5 mm holes and 10 mm countersinks are placeholders inferred from their proportions. The inner ring's relative angle is adjustable.

Overall thickness is **8.5 mm, confirmed by the user**. The 0.6 mm race gap and 2.5 mm countersink depth are also provisional. Hidden raceways, balls, retainers, underside details and machining texture are not reproduced. This is a reference envelope for base design, not a bearing manufacturing model or drilling template. It is not yet inserted into the arm assembly.
