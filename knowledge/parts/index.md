# Printed Part

* [Arm end cap](arm-end-cap.md) - A 20 x 20 x 3 press-fit lid for each cut end of the gantry rail; it carries nothing and is not a travel stop.
* [Camera mount](camera-mount.md) - A light block under the gantry rail with a 1/4-20 tapped hole, replacing the heavier Manfrotto clamp block at the end of the lever.
* [Gantry carriage (hybrid)](gantry-carriage.md) - A printed three-wheel gantry plate bolted to the bought four-wheel metal spine plate; drawn and dropped the same day.
* [Gantry plate](compact-carriage.md) - Printed 8 mm plate joining spine and gantry: four V wheels on the spine, three on the gantry, eccentric spacers for preload.
* [Load stack](load-stack.md) - The counterweight: a printed frame riding the spine's rear slot, holding 50 x 50 x 5 M16 square washers on an M16 bolt.
* [Pulley spine cap](spine-cap-pulley.md) - A 20 x 20 hood over the spine top with a 12 mm pulley inside that drops both runs of the balance line into the front and rear slot channels.
* [Spine cap (V groove)](spine-cap.md) - A 20 x 20 press-fit block with a 45 degree V groove carrying the balance line over the spine top; too much capstan friction.
* [Strip collar](arm-strip-collar.md) - A thin band round the gantry rail that holds a 2 x 15.5 mm steel strip flat along one face, as a track for the magnetic Olight.
* [V base](v-base.md) - Two 300 mm 2020 rails flat on the desk at right angles, with printed angle blocks and a foot holding the bearing's outer race.
* [Washer holder](washer-holder.md) - A 50 x 50 plate with an M16 post, bolted onto a V base rail, to park spare counterweight washers.
* [Weighted base](weight-base.md) - A 1.25 kg dumbbell plate clamped between two printed discs as a freestanding foot; the printed parts bent and it was replaced.
* [Z base](z-base.md) - Printed base on the turntable bearing's inner race that holds the spine's lower end in a 45 mm socket with tee-nut bolts.

# Reference Model

* [Pixel 6a](pixel6a.md) - Reference solid for the phone, with each dimension tagged as published spec or still to be measured.
* [Solid V wheel](vslot-wheel.md) - A nominal Solid V wheel with 625 bearings, for placing wheels at tangency on the V-slot.
* [Turntable bearing](turntable-bearing.md) - Reference model of the salvaged 140 mm lazy-susan bearing; OD and thickness measured, the rest photo estimates.
* [V-slot 2020 profile](vslot-2020.md) - The OpenBuilds 2020 V-slot section modelled from the published drawing, used by every part that touches a rail.
* [Wheel spacers](wheel-spacer.md) - Nominal 6 mm concentric and eccentric spacers; the eccentric offset is 0.395 mm.

# Test Print

* [Base fit test](base-fit-test.md) - A 2 mm gauge plate testing three 2020 socket clearances and both candidate bearing bolt circles in one short print.
* [Belt collar test](belt-collar-test.md) - Two collars that anchor a GT2 belt in a spine slot, for the Z brake that was later set aside.
* [Slide test](slide-test.md) - One T slider on a handle for the load stack's sliding fit in the spine's rear T-slot.
* [Socket test](socket-test.md) - Just the fins Z base's boss and bolt stations, built from z-base.scad's own modules so a pass here is a pass on the base.
