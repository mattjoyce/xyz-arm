The [V base](/parts/v-base.md)'s angle bracket with a wider pad. The post is Ø16 × 33, room
for six washers (0.52 kg). `spares` sets the count. The 15 mm overhang each side carries
nothing, since the steel washers sit on the rail through the 2 mm plate (iteration 60).
