<https://github.com/stevenbennett/Open-Task-Light>. Same spine-and-arm choice of two 500 mm
2020 V-slot rails, arrived at independently. Differs in using a GT2 belt, six wheels and a
steel-shot counterweight slab.

Its carriage STL is kept unmodified in
[`references/open-task-light/`](../../references/open-task-light/README.md) under its MIT
licence, and viewed by `cad/studies/carriage-study.scad`. Analysis:
[`datasheets/prior-art-open-task-light.md`](../../datasheets/prior-art-open-task-light.md).
