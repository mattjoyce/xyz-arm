# Design Decision

* [Balance, don't latch](balance-not-brake.md) - The Z axis is held by matching the counterweight to the payload, not by a belt-and-latch brake that must be squeezed before every move.
* [Braided line as the balance member](braid-not-belt.md) - 0.37 mm PE braid carries the counterweight, chosen over a GT2 belt for pulley size and routing, accepting creep.
* [Camera fixed flat](fixed-flat-camera.md) - The phone looks straight down with no tilt or roll adjustment, so its alignment error is constant and measured once.
* [Counterweight behind the spine](counterweight-behind-spine.md) - The counterweight rides the spine's rear slot on the desk side, rather than hanging through a hole in the desk.
* [Four wheels on the spine](four-spine-wheels.md) - The gantry plate runs four wheels on the spine, two fixed and two eccentric, because three could not hold the arm's moment.
* [Pulley, not groove, at the spine top](pulley-not-groove.md) - A ball-bearing pulley carries the balance line over the spine, because a fixed V groove's capstan friction made the arm stiff.
* [V base, not a weighted base](v-base-not-weighted-base.md) - The arm stands on two 2020 rails in an L rather than on a dumbbell plate between printed discs, because the printed discs bent.
* [Z base on the inner race](inner-race-mount.md) - The Z base bolts to the turntable bearing's inner race; the countersunk outer race is the fixed one.
