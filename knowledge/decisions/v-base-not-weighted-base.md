The [weighted base](/parts/weight-base.md) put 2 mm of printed plastic in bending above and
below the iron. The rig leaned and the under ring bent. The [V base](/parts/v-base.md) makes
the aluminium rails the structure and keeps every printed block small and in compression or
tension through a tee nut.

The rule that came out of it: everything between the bearing's outer race and the desk is
stiff and in compression. PETG is about 35 times floppier than 6063 for the same section.
