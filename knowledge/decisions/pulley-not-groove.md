---
type: Design Decision
title: "Pulley, not groove, at the spine top"
description: "A ball-bearing pulley carries the balance line over the spine, because a fixed V groove's capstan friction made the arm stiff."
timestamp: "2026-09-20T00:00:00Z"
status: settled
tags: [balance, friction]
---

A line turning 180° over a fixed groove pays capstan friction, about 4 to 6 N at 10 N
hanging, against an arm that should float. The [V-groove cap](/parts/spine-cap.md) was rigged
and was too stiff; the [pulley cap](/parts/spine-cap-pulley.md) replaced it the same day.
