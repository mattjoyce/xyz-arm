The [design note](/references/design-note.md) hung a pot of steel shot under the desk on a
line through a hole. The 2026-09-19 session moved everything onto the desk: the line goes
over the [pulley cap](/parts/spine-cap-pulley.md) and down the rear slot to the
[load stack](/parts/load-stack.md), which slides in that slot.

The rear slot also keeps the line close to the rotation axis, so a ±45° sweep moves it
about 4.6 mm instead of 13.8.

Steel square washers replaced shot-filled blocks because they locate on one bolt, carry
about 2.5 times the mass per mm of height of the lead-in-epoxy block, and come off by hand.
